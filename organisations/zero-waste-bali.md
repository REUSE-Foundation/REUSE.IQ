Bali's first bulk food store (opened 2018), stocking 400-700+ organic and non-organic food and eco products with zero packaging. Added a refill-by-delivery service and an in-store DIY product-making station at its fourth (Canggu) location; positions itself as a social enterprise leading Indonesia's zero-waste retail movement.

## Verification Notes

Well-corroborated across 5 independent sources (2019-2024) including Greenpeace, with a consistently named founder (Silvija Rumiha) and opening year (2018). No independently audited impact metrics (e.g. tonnes of plastic avoided) were found in this pass — only qualitative 'first of its kind' framing. UPDATE (second research pass, Aug 2026): The zerowastebali.com domain now resolves to an unrelated gambling/casino site — a classic expired-domain-squatting pattern. However, independent e-commerce analytics (Grips Intelligence) show this domain had genuine, substantial revenue ($11,478 in Feb 2025, 3,818 sessions, 134 transactions) and ZoomInfo/The Punch Community/Made for Planet all list it as an active business with 1-10 employees as recently as May 2024. This strongly suggests the business was genuinely operating within the last 12-18 months but has since lost control of its domain (or ceased trading and the domain lapsed to a squatter) — status is genuinely uncertain, not simply defunct or simply active. Recommend direct verification via Instagram (@zerowastebali) or the physical Kerobokan address (Jl. Raya Anyar No.30) rather than the website. Also flagged: a closely related domain (greenmarketbali.com) shows near-identical business description and franchise language, suggesting these may be the same or sister organisations — see the separate flagged duplicate-avoidance note for "The Green Market Bali."
