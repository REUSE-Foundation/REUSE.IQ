# Vessel

Nonprofit/operator running a reusable cup and container network for coffee shops and foodservice, enabling customers to borrow and return standardized reusable packaging at partner locations.

## Quick Facts

- **Official name:** Vessel Works — source: vesselworks.org; fastcompany.com (2018); bizwest.com (2018)
- **Also known as:** Vessel; trademarked reuse concept 'Freeturnable™' — source: vesselworks.org/home
- **Country / HQ:** United States (Boulder, CO — origin; also Berkeley, CA)
- **Website:** https://vesselworks.org — source: direct
- **Year founded:** Concept developed 2015 by founder Dagny Tucker; piloted in New York City in 2016 (6-month pilot across Manhattan/Brooklyn); ongoing operations in Boulder since 2018 and Berkeley since 2019 — source: greenbrownblue.com; fastcompany.com; bizwest.com
- **Est. company size:** Small startup; structure described (2021) as an education-focused nonprofit that funded a for-profit sister company, 'Vessel Revolution' — source: cobizmag.com (2024, republishing a 2021-era profile)
- **Ownership status:** Founded by Dagny Tucker (previously worked at New Hope Network in Boulder and taught sustainable business at Parsons School of Design, NYC); dual nonprofit/for-profit structure (Vessel Works nonprofit + Vessel Revolution for-profit) per a 2021-era profile; sought $10-20M in investment to scale into markets like Vancouver/San Francisco — source: cobizmag.com
- **Geographic scope:** Boulder, Colorado and Berkeley, California, United States — source: vesselworks.org/faq
- **Categories:** Deposit-return / Reusable packaging
- **REUSE priority rating:** ★★★★ High
- **SDGs:** SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production; SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities

## Organisation Overview

Nonprofit/operator running a reusable cup and container network for coffee shops and foodservice, enabling customers to borrow and return standardized reusable packaging at partner locations.

## Business Model

Free, tech-enabled reusable stainless-steel cup-share service ('Freeturnable™') for cafes: customers sign up (with a credit card charged only if a cup isn't returned), check out a cup via app/QR code, and return it to any participating cafe or return kiosk within the network — source: vesselworks.org/faq; dailycal.org

## Customers

Cafes and coffee shops in Boulder, Colorado (8 food vendors + University of Colorado campus) and Berkeley, California (11 food/beverage vendors, won municipal RFP) — source: greenbrownblue.com; dailycal.org (2019)

## Partnerships

Boxcar Coffee Roasters (named Boulder pilot cafe); University of Colorado (CU) campus; City of Berkeley (won municipal RFP, 2019) — source: fastcompany.com; greenbrownblue.com

## Circular Economy Activities

Deposit-return / Reusable packaging

## Measurable Impact

Boulder, first 6 months of operation (~2018-2019): 4,500+ Vessel cups checked out, 1,260 users signed up, 85% repeat-use rate — source: greenbrownblue.com (2021 profile, republished 2024 context)
