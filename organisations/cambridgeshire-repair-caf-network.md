## Organisation Overview

Regional network, run by charity Cambridge Carbon Footprint, that helps residents organise and run local Repair Café events across Cambridgeshire, connecting them to the global Repair Café movement; partnered with the University of Cambridge to launch dedicated university repair cafés in 2026.

## Business Model

Coordinates and trains a Cambridgeshire-wide network of volunteer-run, free, drop-in Repair Cafe events (electrical, mechanical, bicycle, jewellery, and clothing repair) via a 'How to run a Repair Cafe in your community' training programme, a shared repairer register, and 3 lendable physical repair toolkits (central Cambridge, Soham, Sawston); in 2025-2026 partnered with the University of Cambridge to launch dedicated university repair cafes — source: cambridgecarbonfootprint.org/repair-cafe-organisers-hub; cam.ac.uk (2026)

## Customers

Cambridgeshire residents (and, since 2025-2026, University of Cambridge staff/students) with broken household items needing repair — source: cam.ac.uk (2026)

## Partnerships

South Cambridgeshire District Council; University of Cambridge (2025-2026 partnership); Restarters.net / The Fixometer (national repair-tracking platform); Wessex Insurance — source: cambridgecarbonfootprint.org; therestartproject.org; cam.ac.uk

## Circular Economy Activities

Repair

## Measurable Impact

University of Cambridge's first year of repair cafes (2025, at the West Hub and School of Clinical Medicine): ~80 volunteer participants, 87 volunteer hours, 53 items repaired, 96 kg of waste prevented. Network-wide: 45 organising groups running 75+ events per year across Cambridgeshire — source: cam.ac.uk (2026); cambridgecarbonfootprint.org
