# Re-turn (Ireland DRS)

Operator of Ireland's national Deposit Return Scheme (live since 1 February 2024) for aluminium and PET plastic drink containers (150ml-3L), with deposits of €0.15-€0.25 refunded via retailer redemption points; retailers must accept containers regardless of where purchased.

## Organisation Overview

Operator of Ireland's national Deposit Return Scheme (live since 1 February 2024) for aluminium and PET plastic drink containers (150ml-3L), with deposits of €0.15-€0.25 refunded via retailer redemption points; retailers must accept containers regardless of where purchased.

## Business Model

National deposit-return scheme: consumers pay a deposit of €0.15-€0.25 on covered containers, refunded via reverse-vending machines or manual redemption at any participating retailer regardless of original purchase location — source: en.wikipedia.org

## Customers

All consumers in Ireland purchasing aluminium and PET plastic drink containers (150ml-3L) — source: en.wikipedia.org
