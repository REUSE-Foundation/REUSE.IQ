# Petainer

Manufacturer of reusable PET kegs and containers; a 30-year presence in reusable-PET manufacturing, and an active New Reuse Alliance member advocating for reuse policy (e.g. the EU's Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation, PPWR).

## Quick Facts

- **Official name:** Petainer Ltd - source: zoominfo.com (2022, Ara Partners acquisition press release)
- **Country / HQ:** United Kingdom (London, HQ) - global manufacturing (Sweden, Czech Republic, USA)
- **Website:** https://www.petainer.com - source: direct
- **Year founded:** 2009 (as Petainer, formed via acquisition of the As, Czech Republic and Lidkoping, Sweden manufacturing sites); those sites own history traces to the late 1950s (Lidkoping AB) - source: petainer.com/about
- **Est. company size:** Global operations with manufacturing plants in Sweden, Czech Republic, and the USA (Tennessee, opened 2022), plus blowing facilities in North America, Russia, India, and Australia - source: petpla.net (2023); manufacturing-today.com (2022)
- **Ownership status:** Acquired by Ara Partners (a private-equity firm specialising in industrial decarbonisation) in July 2022; previously backed by KKR, Next Wave Partners, Wheb Partners, and KBC Private Equity; total funding raised $111.4M - source: zoominfo.com (2022); cbinsights.com
- **Geographic scope:** Global - UK headquarters with manufacturing in Sweden, Czech Republic, and USA, sales offices in Germany and USA, blowing facilities in North America, Russia, India, and Australia - source: petpla.net (2023)
- **Categories:** Reusable packaging (B2B, beverage kegs/bottles)
- **REUSE priority rating:** ★★★ Medium
- **SDGs:** SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production

## Organisation Overview

Manufacturer of reusable PET kegs and containers; a 30-year presence in reusable-PET manufacturing, and an active New Reuse Alliance member advocating for reuse policy (e.g. the EU's Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation, PPWR).

## Business Model

Manufactures reusable and single-use PET packaging: refillable PET bottles (RefPET), the petainerKeg (one-way PET keg for beer/beverages), and the petainerCooler (a refillable PET water-cooler bottle); holds leadership positions in the German refillable-PET-bottle market. In January 2024, partnered with Oonly to launch Hungary's first reusable PET bottle for mineral water - source: nextwavepartners.co.uk; cbinsights.com (2024/2025)

## Customers

Beverage, food, and household-product brands globally, including mineral water and soft drink producers, breweries (via petainerKeg), and water-cooler operators (via petainerCooler) - source: petainer.com/about; nextwavepartners.co.uk

## Partnerships

Oonly (Hungary reusable PET bottle launch, Jan 2024); named leader in the German refillable PET bottle market - source: cbinsights.com; nextwavepartners.co.uk
