## Business Model

CATEGORY NUANCE: Packmile manufactures sustainable packaging materials — paper bubble wrap, gummed/water-activated tapes, pallet covers and straps — as alternatives to single-use plastic, polypropylene, thermocol and styrofoam in e-commerce/industrial shipping. Its own marketing describes these as both 'green packaging' and 'reusable packaging,' but the core products (paper-based void-fill and tape) are more accurately biodegradable/recyclable single-use alternatives rather than a genuinely reusable, returnable packaging system — worth the Foundation's judgment on category fit — source: businessindia.co (2020); indiamart.com; packmile.com
