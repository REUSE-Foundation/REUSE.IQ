## Business Model

Cashless, app-free reusable-cup deposit system for venues: a camera-readable invisible code (not RFID) links each cup to the buyer's payment card at purchase; returning the cup at a smart collection kiosk automatically refunds the deposit (e.g. €1.50 at Club Brugge) — source: borro.one; eu-startups.com (2026)

## Customers

Stadiums, football clubs, festivals, concert venues and amusement parks running reusable-cup programmes — source: eu-startups.com; packaginginsights.com

## Measurable Impact

At Club Brugge's Jan Breydel stadium (live since July 2025): 26,000 fans and 45,000 cups processed per matchday; estimated fraud losses reduced by ~€9,889/year and €4,832/year saved in refund transaction fees; company-wide claim of 'millions' of reusable cups processed as of May 2026 — source: borro.one (case study); eu-startups.com (2026)
