## Organisation Overview

Online plastic-free supermarket that delivers groceries, personal-care and household products to customers' homes in returnable packaging and collects empty containers on a subsequent visit — including solid-format products that need no packaging at all.

## Business Model

Online plastic-free supermarket delivering groceries and cleaning products in 100% returnable glass packaging: customers order online, pay a deposit per returnable container, and the company collects used containers at the next delivery (washing them in-house at over 80°C at a facility near Plaza Molina, Barcelona) for redistribution; also offers a B2B recurring-deposit service for offices — source: repotmarket.com; viaempresa.cat (2021); thenewbarcelonapost.com (2022)

## Customers

Individual consumers (age ~28-50, mid-to-high income/education) and SME businesses (via a monthly-deposit office delivery service, likened by founders to 'Aquaservice for SMEs') in Barcelona and surrounding municipalities (L'Hospitalet, Sant Cugat, expanding toward Sant Just Desvern, Badalona, Sant Adrià de Besòs) — source: viaempresa.cat (2021); thenewbarcelonapost.com (2022)

## Partnerships

Le Fourgon (acquirer, April 2026, French reusable-packaging company); TEB (last-mile eco-delivery partner); Koiki (delivery partner employing socially at-risk groups, for the Sant Cugat area) — source: viaempresa.cat (2026); repotmarket.com; thenewbarcelonapost.com (2022)
