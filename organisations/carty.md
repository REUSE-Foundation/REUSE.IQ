French manufacturer of reusable food packaging (round and rectangular 'Cartybox' containers, 'Cartybowl', and secure-closure pots) rated for 50-100+ wash cycles, 40+ freeze cycles and 40+ microwave-heating cycles, 100% recyclable at end of life; markets its products as having a 44% lower CO2 footprint than glass packaging and a much lower footprint than single-use cardboard; serves professional kitchens, on-site dining, takeaway, and home use — source: carty.fr (multiple product pages); cbinsights.com
