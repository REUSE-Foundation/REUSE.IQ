# Bûmerang

Barcelona-founded startup operating a QR/RFID/NFC-based returnable packaging network for restaurant takeaway and delivery. Consumers order via participating restaurants, receive food in Bûmerang containers, and return them within 15 days at any network location for cleaning and reuse. Now works with hospitals, catering companies, and travel-retail operator Áreas at Spanish train stations.

## Organisation Overview

Barcelona-founded startup operating a QR/RFID/NFC-based returnable packaging network for restaurant takeaway and delivery. Consumers order via participating restaurants, receive food in Bûmerang containers, and return them within 15 days at any network location for cleaning and reuse. Now works with hospitals, catering companies, and travel-retail operator Áreas at Spanish train stations.

## Business Model

Returnable-packaging-as-a-service for takeaway/delivery food: reusable bowls (rated for ~200 uses) and cups (~500 uses) supplied to restaurants/caterers; customers have 15 days to return containers to any network point, tracked via QR code, RFID (via a 2024 smart return machine) or NFC (via 'Bumerang Tap', which requires no app) — source: mundosinresiduos.com; interempresas.net (2024)

## Customers

Catering operators, restaurants, cafeterias, hospitals, and hospitality businesses in Spain (B2B), serving individual consumers who order takeaway/delivery food (B2C) — source: corresponsables.com; profesionalhoreca.com

## Partnerships

Grupo Duni (minority investor/hospitality-sector partner, 2021); catering operators, corporate hospitality clients, and small independent food businesses (unnamed) — source: elbiensocial.org

## Measurable Impact

500,000+ single-use containers avoided (as of 2024); 30,000+ registered users, 5,500 active reusers; claimed 30% cost saving for businesses vs. compostable packaging — source: interempresas.net (2024); ineditinnova.com (2024)
