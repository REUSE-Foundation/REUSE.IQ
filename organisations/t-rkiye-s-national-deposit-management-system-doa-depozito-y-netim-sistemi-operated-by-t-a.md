## Business Model

Mandatory national deposit-return system: producers/sellers of covered beverage containers must register; consumers pay a deposit (raised from an initial ₺0.25 pilot incentive to ₺1 per container as of 1 July 2026) and reclaim it by returning containers bearing the DOA logo to reverse-vending machines or redemption points via a QR-code app, with funds credited to a digital wallet redeemable at partner banks/ATMs or stores — source: trthaber.com; depozitoiademakinesi.com

## Customers

All consumers in Turkey purchasing beverages in PET, glass, or aluminium containers between 0.1-3.10 litres — source: dbys.gov.tr

## Partnerships

Partner supermarket and retail chains nationwide serve as redemption points (unnamed specific chains in sources reviewed) — source: trthaber.com

## Circular Economy Activities

Deposit-return

## Measurable Impact

Targets ~25 billion containers/year recovered and an estimated ₺30 billion/year direct contribution to the economy, per official government projections (not yet realised/audited results, as the scheme only went fully nationwide 1 July 2026) — source: trthaber.com
