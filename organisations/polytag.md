## Organisation Overview

Digital-tracking technology company whose 'Digital Link' QR codes are used on Ocado Retail's reusable packaging ranges to guide consumers through refill-and-return steps as part of a digital deposit-return scheme; over 60 product ranges are enabled.

## Business Model

Connected-packaging and recycling-traceability technology: invisible UV watermarks and GS1-powered QR codes are applied to packaging (e.g. milk cartons), readable by Polytag's network of 'Plastic Detection Units' (PDUs) installed in Material Recovery Facilities (MRFs) across the UK and Northern Ireland, giving brands item-level, real-time data on where and when packaging is actually recycled. Marketed jointly with GS1 UK as an enabler of 'Digital Deposit Return Schemes' (DDRS), and as of 2023 added a consumer rewards layer offering 20p per recycled item scanned — source: circularonline.co.uk; gs1uk.org

## Customers
