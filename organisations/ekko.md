# Ekko

One of at least five Canadian startups (alongside A Friendlier Company, Suppli, Reusables.com, Re-Vita) offering reusable, returnable takeout containers for restaurants and food delivery, charging a per-order fee (50 cents-$2) and tracking containers via QR code or electronic ID tag for return within 1-2 weeks.

## Organisation Overview

One of at least five Canadian startups (alongside A Friendlier Company, Suppli, Reusables.com, Re-Vita) offering reusable, returnable takeout containers for restaurants and food delivery, charging a per-order fee (50 cents-$2) and tracking containers via QR code or electronic ID tag for return within 1-2 weeks.

## Business Model

Reusable takeout container service: restaurants order stainless-steel containers (3 sizes) from Ekko; customers request their order in an Ekko container and are charged a small service fee ($0.50/container or a $2 flat rate, depending on the restaurant partner), identified via their phone number as an 'Ekko ID'; containers are returned to drop-box locations within about a week, then collected, sanitized in a commercial dishwashing facility, and redistributed to partner restaurants — source: ekkoreuse.com/faqs; helloekko.ca/for-restaurants

## Customers

Restaurants and food vendors in Waterloo Region, Ontario, and their takeout customers — source: helloekko.ca/for-restaurants

## Partnerships

Wooden Boat Food Company, Underground Flavour Group (named restaurant partners in a 2021 feature) — source: communitech.ca (2021)

## Circular Economy Activities

Deposit-return / Reusable packaging

## Measurable Impact

In the first two days after its April 2021 launch, Ekko replaced 231 single-use takeout containers with reusables — described as roughly enough to stack to the height of a four-storey building — source: plasticactioncentre.ca (2021)
