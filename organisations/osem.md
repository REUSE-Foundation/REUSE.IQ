## Organisation Overview

Plastic-free grocery store in Bloemfontein selling a wide range of foodstuffs sold by weight for container-free shopping.

## Business Model

Described as the first zero-waste shop of its kind in Bloemfontein: customers bring their own empty containers, fill them with desired products (as little or as much as needed), and pay by weight; the shop prioritises local sourcing while maintaining quality — source: bloemfonteincourant.co.za (2021)

## Customers

Bloemfontein residents wanting to reduce single-use plastic packaging waste — source: bloemfonteincourant.co.za (2021)
