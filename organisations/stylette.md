## Organisation Overview

Kids' clothing rental platform: purchases inventory wholesale from children's fashion brands (e.g. Nelly Stella, Tutu Du Monde, Luna Luna) and rents pieces to parents, extending garment life in a segment with high size-turnover.

## Business Model

Online rental (and increasingly resale) marketplace for children's designer clothing: parents select items from a curated catalog (dresses, coats, jackets, suits, accessories, mostly under $100 to rent), choose a 5-day or 10-day rental period, and return items afterward or purchase them; also offers a monthly membership renting 2-4 outfits/month. Brand partners include Nelly Stella, Tutu Du Monde and Luna Luna, currently purchased at wholesale with a move toward commission-based brand partnerships planned — source: labusinessjournal.com (2023); techweek.com

## Customers

Parents of children aged approximately 6 months to 10 years, for special-occasion wear (flower girl dresses, suits) and increasingly everyday wear — source: labusinessjournal.com (2023)

## Partnerships

Brand partners Nelly Stella, Tutu Du Monde, and Luna Luna; investor/accelerator Expert Dojo (per Crunchbase) — source: labusinessjournal.com; crunchbase.com
