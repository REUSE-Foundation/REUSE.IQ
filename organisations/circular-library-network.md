# Circular Library Network

Software and peer-support network (built on the myTurn platform) enabling self-checkout 'libraries of things' and tool libraries worldwide; originated from the Reykjavik Tool Library's need to automate lending during the pandemic.

## Organisation Overview

Software and peer-support network (built on the myTurn platform) enabling self-checkout 'libraries of things' and tool libraries worldwide; originated from the Reykjavik Tool Library's need to automate lending during the pandemic.

## Business Model

Modular, open-design self-checkout hardware and software system enabling 'libraries of things' — communities/organisations can lend tools, household items and small electronics via a computerised interface; hardware can be manufactured locally or built into existing furniture (e.g. repurposed IKEA units) for decentralised, low-cost deployment — source: supplychainshift.substack.com (2024); definite-ccri.eu

## Customers

Libraries, community hubs, supermarkets, co-working spaces, real estate operators, universities and municipalities worldwide wanting to run self-checkout tool/item-lending systems — source: definite-ccri.eu

## Partnerships

myTurn.com (technology partner, founder Gene Homicki); RANNIS (Icelandic Research Fund); Village Capital Inc; DEFINITE-CCRI (EU circular-economy investment programme) — source: shareable.net; f6s.com; definite-ccri.eu

## Circular Economy Activities

Rental (enabling platform/network)

## Measurable Impact

Reykjavik pilot project results: 42 tools lent, borrowed 254 times in the first 4 months; expanded from 3 to 7 active Icelandic locations (funding secured for 4 additional); increased partner library memberships by 3% yearly; saved users a total of €10,150; kept 1.2 tonnes of CO2 and 1.9 tonnes of e-waste out of landfills — source: circularlibrary.network/pilot-project
