## Organisation Overview

Baby-clothing rental subscription service: parents pay monthly only while using selected items, shipped and returned in a reusable Borobabi bag; founded by Carolyn Butler, drawing on prior experience in lean manufacturing and supply-chain logistics.

## Business Model

Circular rental/resale retailer for maternity and children's clothing: parents order and keep items until their child outgrows them (paying a monthly fee while in use), then either purchase at a reduced price or return items (incentivized via store credit / a 20% 'Give Back' reward) for the garment to be cleaned, restored, and recirculated to another family; only garments from brands using organic/certified natural inputs (GOTS, OEKO-TEX) are used, with end-of-life recycling/composting for garments that can no longer be reused — source: wwd.com (2022); thebusinessmagnate.com (2024)

## Customers

Parents of infants and young children in the US seeking sustainable, organic children's clothing via rent/buy-back model — source: entrepreneurship.columbia.edu

## Partnerships

Brand partners included Nui, Mori, Nature Baby, and Liilu — source: sustainablebrands.com (2021)

## Circular Economy Activities

Rental

## Measurable Impact

As of ~2 years post-launch (per a March 2022 B Corp certification article): saved 39 metric tons of CO2 emissions, 10.7 million gallons of water, and negated the consumption of 2 metric tons of virgin cotton through its rental operations — source: wwd.com (Mar 2022)
