# ReMarket

Described as Uruguay's first package-free ('libre de envases') store — a 100%-circular ecological grocery and household-goods shop founded by two women entrepreneurs, offering 400+ products sold in bulk or in returnable/compostable packaging, with nationwide Uruguay shipping.

- **Country / HQ:** Uruguay (Montevideo — Pocitos)
- **Website:** https://www.remarket.com.uy — not independently re-fetched this pass

## Organisation Overview

Described as Uruguay's first package-free ('libre de envases') store — a 100%-circular ecological grocery and household-goods shop founded by two women entrepreneurs, offering 400+ products sold in bulk or in returnable/compostable packaging, with nationwide Uruguay shipping.

## Business Model

Uruguay's first package-free store: customers buy the same categories of goods as a conventional supermarket (except initially fresh produce) in bulk, either bringing their own reusable containers or using free recycled-paper bags; online ordering with home delivery is also available. Prioritises Uruguayan-made products to reduce carbon footprint and support local producers. Cloth bags are supplied by Ceprodih (Centro de promoción por la dignidad humana), a nonprofit supporting women in at-risk situations through a circular-economy programme that reuses industrial waste materials — source: infonegocios.biz (2021); elobservador.com.uy (Oct 2021)

## Customers

Uruguayan consumers wanting to buy the full range of typical supermarket categories (food, grains, flours, condiments, cleaning products) package-free, at prices comparable to conventional retail — source: infonegocios.biz (2021)

## Partnerships

Ceprodih (nonprofit supplier of cloth bags, supporting women in vulnerable situations via a circular-economy materials-reuse programme) — source: elobservador.com.uy (Oct 2021)
