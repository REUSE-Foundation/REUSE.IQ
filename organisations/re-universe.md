## Organisation Overview

Digital reuse platform for cup and container deposit-return, used at sites including Blenheim Palace and the Eden Project. Offers both a classic paid-deposit model and a card-based 'holding charge' model (no upfront payment, charge applied only if not returned within 7 days); each container carries RFID for tracking through wash/return cycles.

## Business Model

Digital returns/reuse platform: venues choose between a classic deposit model or a card-based 'holding charge' (no upfront payment, charge applied only if the cup isn't returned within a set period); each cup is tracked via the platform, integrated with partner hardware (Circular&Co. cups, Cuploop return machines) and payment rails (Mastercard Send, Shift4) for instant deposit refunds via tap-to-return kiosks — source: re-universe.com (2024)

## Customers

UK visitor attractions and hospitality venues (e.g. Blenheim Palace, Eden Project); UK supermarkets are reported to have visited to evaluate the system — source: edenproject.com; museumsandheritage.com (2024)

## Measurable Impact

Combined across Blenheim Palace and Eden Project: ~620,000 fewer single-use coffee cups per year, ~27 tonnes CO2e/year saved (equivalent to heating 12 homes for a year per Eden Project's own comparison). Eden Project alone: ~220,000 cups/year avoided, ~4 tonnes waste eliminated, 72% CO2e saving. Company-wide claim: return rates as high as 96% — source: museumsandheritage.com (2024); edenproject.com (2024); re-universe.com
