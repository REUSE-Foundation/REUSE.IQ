## Organisation Overview

Manages supermarket bottle-return depots and handles export of glass and plastic bottles as part of Barbados's long-running (1986) national deposit-refund system under the Returnable Containers Act.

## Business Model

CATEGORY NUANCE (same as flagged for Recycling Partners Jamaica): this is a recycling/scrap buy-back and export operation, not a returnable-container reuse system. B's Recycling manages supermarket bottle-return depots under Barbados' national container-recycling legislation (in place since 1992, updated 2019) and handles export of collected glass and plastic bottles; the company also buys/collects a much broader range of scrap materials (metals, cardboard, appliances, used vehicles) unrelated to beverage containers specifically — source: bottlebill.org; linkedin.com/company/bs-recycling

## Customers

All Barbados residents and businesses with recyclable bottles, containers, metals, cardboard, appliances, or vehicles; free collection service available for those unable to visit a depot — source: linkedin.com/company/bs-recycling; barbadosdigital.com

## Partnerships

Operates within Barbados' national container-recycling legislative framework (Returnable Containers Act, 1992/2019 update); metal containers specifically are handled by a separate operator, Recycling Preparation Inc — source: bottlebill.org
