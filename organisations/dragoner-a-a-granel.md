## Organisation Overview

Modern 'drugstore' offering bulk cleaning and hygiene products; runs a container delivery-and-pickup system for home customers (reusing containers) and supplies bulk 10L/15L boxes to consumer groups ('grupos de consumo').

## Business Model

Physical zero-waste 'droguería' (drugstore) at C. del Carmen 9, Cercedilla, Madrid, selling household cleaning and personal-care products in bulk; customers bring their own containers or buy them in-store to refill. Explicitly does NOT sell bottled/pre-packaged liquid products (cleaning products, shower gels, liquid shampoos) via its online store as a matter of philosophy — these are only available in-person at the physical shop or via WhatsApp/email orders with a container delivery-and-pickup/reuse system for local customers; also supplies bulk 10L/15L boxes to consumer cooperative groups — source: dragoneriagranel.es; dragoneriagranel.es/contacto

## Customers

Local residents of Cercedilla and nearby Madrid-region towns; also serves 'grupos de consumo' (local food-buying cooperatives) with bulk 10L/15L container supply — source: dragoneriagranel.es
