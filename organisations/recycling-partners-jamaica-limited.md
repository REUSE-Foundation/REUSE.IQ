## Organisation Overview

Entity selected to implement Jamaica's national plastic-bottle deposit-refund scheme, paying consumers a minimum J$1 for each PET or HDPE bottle returned to depots across the island; funded via a producer-paid per-bottle cess.

## Business Model

CATEGORY NUANCE: this is fundamentally a recycling buy-back/deposit-REFUND scheme rather than a reusable-packaging system — consumers/collectors bring PET or HDPE plastic bottles to RPJ depots and receive a cash payment (currently $50 per kilogram, ~$22/lb; earlier rates were as low as $1-10 per bottle in 2019-2020) for bottles which are then recycled (not reused in their original form) into products like carpet fibre and synthetic materials. Jamaica's DRS is explicitly described by RPJ's own blog as an 'implied' rather than point-of-sale deposit model (unlike Scandinavian-style DRS where the deposit is added to the purchase price and refunded on return) — source: recyclingja.com (blog, 'Jamaica's Plastic Bottle DRS Explained'); recyclingja.com/become-a-recycler

## Customers

All Jamaican consumers and any person/organisation (except government agencies) wishing to monetise plastic-bottle recycling — source: recyclingja.com (2024/2025 blog)

## Partnerships

Jamaican plastic bottle manufacturers/bottlers (funding the scheme via self-imposed cess); Bureau of Standards Jamaica (BSJ); Ministry of Economic Growth and Job Creation — source: jamaicaobserver.com (2019); jamaica-gleaner.com (2022)

## Circular Economy Activities

Deposit-return (recycling-oriented, not reuse) — recommend the Foundation reassess category fit given the core-model nuance above

## Measurable Impact

FY2018/2019: 2.3 million pounds of plastic bottles recovered, with 6 additional depots commissioned that year. As of a 2022 government statement, less than 20% of newly produced bottles were being collected for recycling nationally, against a national target of ~60% (this figure reflects the broader Jamaican recycling landscape, not solely RPJ's performance) — source: jamaica-gleaner.com (Jan 2020; Jun 2022)

## Verification Notes

Extremely well-documented across 7 independent sources spanning 2019-2025 (Bottle Bill Resource Guide, Jamaica Gleaner x3, Jamaica Observer, Magnetic Media, Jamaica Information Service, plus the company's own site), all consistent on launch timeline, funding mechanism, and current payout rate. IMPORTANT CATEGORY FLAG: this scheme pays for bottles to be RECYCLED (turned into new materials), not reused in their original form as a returnable container — this is a meaningfully different model from consumer-facing DRS systems like Germany's Pfand or the new Turkey/Singapore/Ireland schemes also in this dataset, which return the SAME container to circulation. Recommend the Foundation explicitly decide whether recycling-oriented buy-back schemes fit their reuse-focused mission before finalizing this entry's categorisation.
