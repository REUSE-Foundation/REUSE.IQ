# Saruga Pack-Free Shopping Store

Package-free bulk shopping store noted as planning further Jakarta expansion; part of a small cluster of zero-waste bulk stores across Jakarta, Surabaya and Bali.

## Quick Facts

- **Official name:** Saruga Pack Free Store — source: nowjakarta.co.id (2022); waste4change.com
- **Country / HQ:** Indonesia (Jakarta — Bintaro)
- **Website:** https://www.sarugapackfreestore.id — source: goinggreeninjakarta.org
- **Year founded:** November 2018 — source: thejakartapost.com (2020); waste4change.com; nowjakarta.co.id (2022) — all consistent
- **Est. company size:** Small independent shop, founder-operated — source: waste4change.com
- **Ownership status:** Founded by Adi Asmawan, who built the store out of personal concern over neighbourhood waste-burning pollution — source: thejakartapost.com (2020); waste4change.com
- **Geographic scope:** Bintaro, South Jakarta, Indonesia — specific address confirmed: Jalan Taman Bintaro #1, Sektor 1 — source: goinggreeninjakarta.org; waste4change.com
- **Categories:** Refill
- **REUSE priority rating:** ★★★ Medium
- **SDGs:** SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production

## Organisation Overview

Package-free bulk shopping store noted as planning further Jakarta expansion; part of a small cluster of zero-waste bulk stores across Jakarta, Surabaya and Bali.

## Business Model

Described as the first zero-waste store in South Jakarta: customers bring their own containers to buy 300+ items (staples, spices, cleaning products, personal-care items) by weight/gram; also sells lifestyle products like stainless-steel straws — source: nowjakarta.co.id (2022); flokq.com; goinggreeninjakarta.org

## Customers

South Jakarta residents seeking package-free pantry staples, seasonings, personal care and home care products — source: nowjakarta.co.id (2022)
