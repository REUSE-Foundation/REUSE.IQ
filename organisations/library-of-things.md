# Library of Things

Circular-economy platform enabling communities, councils and businesses to run 'libraries of things' — locker- and hub-based rental of tools, DIY equipment, camping gear and kitchen appliances (sourced from partners like Bosch and STIHL) — reducing the need to buy rarely-used items.

## Organisation Overview

Circular-economy platform enabling communities, councils and businesses to run 'libraries of things' — locker- and hub-based rental of tools, DIY equipment, camping gear and kitchen appliances (sourced from partners like Bosch and STIHL) — reducing the need to buy rarely-used items.

## Business Model

Circular-economy rental platform: locker/kiosk-based self-service lending of tools, DIY equipment, camping gear and kitchen appliances, sourced partly from manufacturer partners; users pay a small daily/rental fee, with concessionary pricing for low-income members — source: lbbd.gov.uk (2022)

## Customers

UK residents, councils, and community organisations wanting to borrow tools, DIY equipment, and household items instead of buying them — source: lbbd.gov.uk (2022); cooperativecity.org

## Partnerships

Product manufacturer partners Bosch, Kärcher and STIHL; multiple UK local councils including Barking & Dagenham; City of London (2026 site launch, per prior research in this conversation) — source: lbbd.gov.uk (2022)

## Circular Economy Activities

Rental

## Measurable Impact

As of 2022 (single-borough figures): 6,000+ people borrowed items 10,000+ times, saving ~60 tonnes of landfill waste and ~120 tonnes of CO2 emissions; UK-wide as of Sept 2025: almost 40,000 members and 70,400+ rentals to date — source: lbbd.gov.uk (2022); cooperativecity.org (2025)
