## Business Model

Social-franchise network: local entrepreneurs co-invest (~$1,000) to operate a Jibu water-purification and distribution franchise; customers buy a reusable 20-litre polycarbonate bottle once ($9-12, rated for 200-400+ refills) then pay only for refills (~$0.07/litre) — source: ssir.org (2021 podcast); circle-economy.com; unreasonablegroup.com

## Customers

Low-income urban households in East/Central Africa purchasing affordable purified drinking water; ~40% of franchises women-owned — source: waterunite.org (2022)

## Partnerships

Water Unite Impact (impact investor, $500k in 2022); no other named corporate/NGO partners confirmed in this pass — source: waterunite.org

## Measurable Impact

2021: 102 million litres of water supplied to 380,000+ daily beneficiaries; cumulative franchisee turnover of $12M+ from water sales; 400M+ litres sold since inception at an average $0.07/litre — source: waterunite.org (2022), wellersimpact.com (2022)
