## Organisation Overview

Manufactures and operates reusable industrial packaging (collapsible plastic containers, foldable crates, pallets, dunnage) for automotive, retail and logistics supply chains, with a proprietary 'Trakkia' barcode/RFID platform for real-time asset tracking across reuse cycles.

## Business Model

'Pooling' rental model for industrial reusable packaging (collapsible plastic containers, foldable crates, PP boxes, dunnage, pallets, wiremesh containers) as an alternative to single-use corrugated boxes — described by the company as 'Uber for industrial packaging.' Assets are serialised and tracked via barcodes and RFID tags through its proprietary 'Trakkia' platform, providing real-time visibility on allotment, dehire and pool distribution — source: yantrapacks.com; crunchbase.com

## Customers

Industrial supply-chain businesses across automotive, retail/commerce, food, and logistics sectors, serviced across major customer/OEM locations in India (offices/service areas named: Gurugram, Chennai, Bangalore, Delhi) — source: yantrapacks.com; tracxn.com
