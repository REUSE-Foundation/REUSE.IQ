Sells detergent, dishwashing liquid and fabric-softener refills for reusable containers, targeting household cleaning-product waste reduction.

## Organisation Overview

Sells detergent, dishwashing liquid and fabric-softener refills for reusable containers, targeting household cleaning-product waste reduction.

## Business Model

Dispenser-based refill stations for cleaning products (detergent, fabric softener, dishwashing liquid, all-purpose/floor cleaner) in physical retail locations across the Valparaíso region and Santiago (Mall Plaza Vespucio); each reusable container carries a QR code linked to the user's account, allowing the company to track refill counts and quantify each customer's 'plastic savings'; containers can be reused up to ~10 times per year and are replaced/recycled by the company when worn out; a subscription/delivery model was also launched — source: elmostrador.cl; paiscircular.cl (2019); df.cl (2019)

## Customers

Chilean consumers (Valparaíso region initially, expanding to Santiago/Region Metropolitana) buying refills of cleaning products — source: paiscircular.cl (2019); df.cl (2019)

## Partnerships

Empresas Demaria (parent/affiliated company via brands Virginia, Igenix, Aroma) — source: df.cl (2019)

## Circular Economy Activities

Refill / Deposit-return

## Measurable Impact

As of Nov 2019 (first ~8 months of operation cited in one source; full-year 2019 figures in another): approximately 600 tonnes of product sold (~200,000 refills) to ~20,000 customers; avoided an estimated 6 tonnes of plastic packaging per year; a separate figure states 12 tonnes of plastic and 5 tonnes of CO2 saved over 8 months; average consumer saves ~35 plastic containers per year — source: paiscircular.cl (2019); elmostrador.cl (2019/2023)
