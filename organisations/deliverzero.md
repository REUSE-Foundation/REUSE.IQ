## Organisation Overview

Reusable takeout container network operating in partnership with restaurants and food-delivery platforms in US cities; customers receive orders in reusable containers and return them at drop-off points or via delivery-app integration for washing and reuse.

## Business Model

Returnable reusable packaging platform for restaurants and delivery: customers order via the DeliverZero app/website or partner delivery apps, receive food in reusable containers, and return them to a participating restaurant or the next delivery courier; company also licenses tracking/logistics/sanitization technology to other foodservice businesses — source: cnn.com; cbinsights.com

## Customers

New York City restaurants and their takeout/delivery customers; expanded to broader US restaurant/foodservice businesses adopting reusable packaging technology (tracking, logistics, sanitization) — source: cnn.com; cbinsights.com

## Partnerships

135+ NYC restaurant partners (as of 2021); investors Fine Day Ventures, Feldsmith Capital — source: cnn.com; cbinsights.com
