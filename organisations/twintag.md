## Organisation Overview

Digital-ID and tracking platform for reusable packaging (QR/RFID-based), enabling brands to run B2B and B2C reusable-packaging-as-a-service programmes with traceability across the wash/return cycle.

## Business Model

Digital-ID platform ('twintags') creating a 'digital twin' for physical products, assets, or packaging via QR code, RFID, or NFC, enabling traceability, digital product passports, and reusable-packaging/deposit-return use cases. For reusable packaging specifically, twintags track each unit through its lifecycle (production, use, return, cleaning, redistribution), counting reuse cycles toward a certified maximum and monitoring wear — enabling 'Packaging-as-a-Service' models without requiring an app or account (scan-only). Active reusable-packaging pilot projects include 'Boemerang' (Belgian reusable packaging initiative), 'Retourpack' (reusable six-pack beer crates with DW Reusables, Carrefour, and Royal Swinkels brewery), and a Digital Deposit Return System (DDRS) pilot with Verhaert and Cegeka initiated by OVAM (Flemish public waste agency) — source: twintag.com/blog (Oct 2024); twintag.com/press; twintag.com/use-cases/reusable-packaging
