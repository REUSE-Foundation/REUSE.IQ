## Organisation Overview

One of the pioneers (dating to 1967) of pooled reusable plastic packaging for the beverage industry — plastic layer pads, reusable pallets and top caps for glass/PET/can transport — operating a 100% full-service pooling system with washing, repair and redistribution.

## Business Model

CATEGORY NUANCE: this is a B2B industrial reusable-packaging pooling company (reusable transport packaging/RTP), not a consumer-facing single-use-plastic-reduction operator. Cartonplast manufactures and manages a full-service closed-loop pool of reusable plastic layer pads, pallets, and top caps used to protect glass, can, and PET containers during transport from container manufacturers to fillers; the company handles stocking, sorting, cleaning, delivery and collection, with 45 million+ RTP units in circulation and 140 million+ cycles per year (per 2018 figures) — source: packagingconnections.com (2018); cartonplast.com/en/services

## Customers

Beverage, food, pharmaceutical and cosmetic industry fillers/manufacturers (glass, can, and PET container producers) across 20-30 countries — source: packagingconnections.com (2018); empackmadrid.com (2024)

## Partnerships

Acquired Green Plate GmbH (Germany) in September 2018 to strengthen its returnable transport packaging capabilities; historic collaboration with Heineken in the 1980s helped develop the reusable PP layer pad product; also worked with the Zanussi Group (refrigerator components) — source: packagingconnections.com (2018); karton.it
