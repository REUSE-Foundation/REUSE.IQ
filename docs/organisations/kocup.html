Organisation Overview

Manufacturer and operator of a reusable-cup deposit system ('ÖkoCup Share') for canteens, cafeterias and restaurants: a barcode/GTIN on each cup handles issue and return, with a QR code directing users to other participating 'Share' partners for cross-location returns.

Business Model

Manufactures and operates a reusable-cup/bowl deposit system: barcode/GTIN-tracked cups (also available with optional reverse-vending deposit machines) made in Berlin from recycled/EU-sourced plastic, rated for 800-1,000 wash cycles (~2 years), then recycled into new cups (90%) or other products like flower pots (10%) — source: oekocup.de; unzerbrechbar.de (product spec sheet)

Customers

Arenas/event venues, university campuses, company canteens, warehouses, supermarkets, and clinics — source: oekocup.de/en/reusable-system; eishockey-magazin.de

Partnerships

AEG (Anschutz Entertainment Group) Germany — named venue client operating Barclays Arena (Hamburg) and Mercedes-Benz Arena/Verti Music Hall (Berlin) — source: eishockey-magazin.de (2022)

Circular Economy Activities

Deposit-return

Measurable Impact

AEG's specific rollout: ~172,000 ÖkoCups (0.25L/0.3L/0.5L sizes) supplied, with venue-based washing lines powered by green electricity to avoid transport emissions to external wash facilities — source: eishockey-magazin.de (2022)
