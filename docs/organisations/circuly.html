Organisation Overview

Subscription- and rental-management SaaS platform enabling brands (bikes/scooters, baby & kids' goods, electronics & appliances, medical equipment) to launch product-as-a-service and rental business models, including recurring billing and reverse-logistics support; partnered with logistics firm Posti.

Business Model

B2B SaaS platform enabling e-commerce businesses to launch and manage subscription/rental (product-as-a-service) models for physical products alongside traditional sales, via a white-label checkout supporting both purchase and rental in one flow; automates recurring billing, invoicing, asset-lifecycle tracking, product returns/refurbishment, dunning, fraud prevention, and reverse logistics; operates across 17-18 countries — source: startupintros.com; circuly.io/about-circuly

Customers

E-commerce retailers and manufacturers across baby products, bikes/e-bikes, electronics, mobility (scooters), and furniture wanting to add rental/subscription (product-as-a-service) alongside traditional sales — source: dealroom.co; startupintros.com

Partnerships

Named client companies: Bugaboo, Paceheads, Riese & Müller (bikes), Fahrrad.de, Tier (scooter rental) — source: startupintros.com; startbase.com (2021)
