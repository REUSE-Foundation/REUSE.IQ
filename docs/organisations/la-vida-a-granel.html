Organisation Overview

Described as the first zero-waste-focused store in the Ecuadorian Amazon, based in Tena; aims to reduce plastic consumption via reuse/recycling of materials for everyday consumer goods, alongside environmental-education and fair-trade goals for the local community (an estimated 60,880 Tena-area residents).

Business Model

Described as the first zero-waste-focused store in the Ecuadorian Amazon; sells food, personal-care, and household-cleaning products in bulk, package-free, prioritising local and biodegradable products, while promoting local entrepreneurs and environmental education for the Tena community (population ~60,880 per an MIT Solve profile) — source: solve.mit.edu; vidagraneldeltena.com/quienes-somos

Customers

Residents of Tena and the surrounding Ecuadorian Amazon region seeking package-free food, personal-care and household products — source: vidagraneldeltena.com/quienes-somos

Refill / Zero-waste retail

Measurable Impact

Contextual data (not company-specific): in Ecuador, 53% of waste goes to open-air dumps, 23.3% to controlled landfills, 20% to sanitary landfills, and 4.1% to rivers/incinerators; in Tena specifically, waste is deposited in a single open-air dump with no solid-waste separation — these are national/regional baseline statistics motivating the store's founding, not the store's own achieved impact — source: solve.mit.edu
