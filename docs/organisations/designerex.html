Organisation Overview

Peer-to-peer occasion-wear rental marketplace ('Airbnb for dresses') allowing users to rent designer dresses from one another rather than buying single-occasion garments; expanded into the US market in 2019.

Business Model

Peer-to-peer designer dress rental marketplace ('Airbnb for dresses'): individual dress owners and rental businesses list designer dresses; renters browse, compare, and book with real-time ID verification and a two-way review system; lenders set their own fee and pay no listing cost, only a service fee upon an accepted booking; supports 'rent now, pay later' via Afterpay/Zip — source: startupdaily.net; linkedin.com/company/designerex

Customers

Women seeking designer dresses/occasion wear for events (weddings, formals, cocktail parties) in Australia and, following a 2022 raise, expanding to the UK/Europe and US — source: insideretail.com.au (2022); linkedin.com/company/designerex

Partnerships

PixelForce (2022 investor, also behind Kayla Itsines' 'Sweat' fitness app) — source: insideretail.com.au (2022)
