My Best Life CNX

Organisation Overview

Zero-waste refill shop and charity shop in Chiang Mai run under an organisation supporting adult refugee students at Thai Freedom House; sells refillable shampoos/soaps and reduces plastic waste while funding refugee education programmes.

Business Model

Combined zero-waste refill shop, vegan café, and charity shop: sells refillable shampoo/soap, vegan dishes, and secondhand clothing/household items (donated or for sale), with all profits directed to the Thai Freedom House scholarship fund for refugee students — source: khaosodenglish.com (2020)

Customers

Chiang Mai residents seeking refillable shampoos/soaps, vegan food, and secondhand clothing/household items — source: khaosodenglish.com (2020); bangkokpost.com (2019)
