Organisation Overview

Subscription clothing-rental service for babies and young children: parents pay a subscription to receive a rotating supply of organic children's clothing that is returned, cleaned and reused as children outgrow sizes.

Business Model

Circular subscription (rental) model for maternity and baby/toddler clothing: for a monthly fee (originally ~$55/£42 for a 16-20 item bundle), subscribers receive organic-cotton clothing in their child's current size; as the child grows, the package is exchanged for the next size up, and returned items are chip-tracked, sorted, inspected, repaired if needed, and industrially laundered (via an independent laundry partner) before recirculating to the next family — each garment is designed to be rented out up to ~100-150 times rather than the 5-20 uses typical of ordinary baby clothes — source: fashnerd.com (2017); marieclaire.com.au (2023); circitnord.com (2020)

Customers

Expectant parents and parents of babies/toddlers in Denmark (maternity and children's wear subscribers) — source: littlescandinavian.com; launch.org

Partnerships

Kolding School of Design (Denmark) partnered on a formal research & development project studying the Vigga service, laundry habits, and design aesthetics — source: designskolenkolding.dk

Circular Economy Activities

Rental

Measurable Impact

~3,000 members in Denmark (as of ~2017, joining since 2014); each garment designed for ~100-150 uses versus ~5-20 for typically-purchased baby clothes; subscription cost reported by BBC as roughly half the price of buying 6 new bodysuits at retail — source: fashnerd.com (2017)
