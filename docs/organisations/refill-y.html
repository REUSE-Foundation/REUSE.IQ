Organisation Overview

B2B refill service delivering household product refills (in partnership with manufacturers) to SME offices and hospitality businesses, aiming to reduce the volume of single-use plastic bottles/containers discarded monthly by those businesses.

Business Model

Hybrid retail/delivery refill model combining two existing ideas — motorbike delivery and refill stores — to solve the accessibility problem of traditional refill shops (which require customers to travel to a physical store with containers, and often stock unfamiliar all-natural brands). Refill Đây instead delivers refills of well-known, trusted household product brands directly to customers' homes or offices, aiming to reduce the number of plastic bottles/containers discarded monthly by SMEs and households toward zero; also creates job opportunities for women and lower-income workers — source: vietnamcirculareconomy.vn (2023); refillvn.com/index.php/about-us

Customers

SME offices and hospitality businesses, plus households, in Ho Chi Minh City — source: refillvn.com/index.php/about-us
