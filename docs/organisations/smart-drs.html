Organisation Overview

Automated, fully self-service reusable-cup return system for stadiums, festivals and other high-traffic venues: visitors drop cups at a 'Smart Collection Point' and receive their deposit back instantly to their payment card, without registration, an app, or queueing. Deployed at all arenas for the 2026 IIHF U18 World Championship.

Business Model

Fully automated, AI-powered deposit-return system for both single-use beverage packaging and reusable cups/containers: an AI camera visually identifies returned cups by shape, dimensions and design profile (no tag, chip, or QR code required on the cup itself), enabling staff-free returns with instant deposit refunds direct to the visitor's payment card, via 'Smart Collection Point' return stations placed in high-traffic venue areas — source: smartdrs.com; smartdrs.com/smart-reusable-cups

Customers

Sports stadiums, festivals, events, corporate campuses, and universities running reusable cup or deposit-return schemes — source: smartdrs.com/smart-reusable-cups

Partnerships

2026 IIHF Ice Hockey U18 World Championship (named event deployment, Slovakia) — source: waste-management-world.com (May 2026)

Measurable Impact

At the 2026 IIHF U18 World Championship: 23,000 reusable cups in circulation across 29 games over 9 match days; more than 55,000 beverages served in reusable cups; over 44,300 cup returns processed via automated stations; nearly 5,000 cups collected daily; an estimated 70,000-80,000 single-use plastic cups avoided — source: smartdrs.com/reusable-cups-stadiums (Jun 2026); waste-management-world.com (May 2026)
