Organisation Overview

Manufacturer of reusable PET kegs and containers; a 30-year presence in reusable-PET manufacturing, and an active New Reuse Alliance member advocating for reuse policy (e.g. the EU's Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation, PPWR).

Business Model

Manufactures reusable and single-use PET packaging: refillable PET bottles (RefPET), the petainerKeg (one-way PET keg for beer/beverages), and the petainerCooler (a refillable PET water-cooler bottle); holds leadership positions in the German refillable-PET-bottle market. In January 2024, partnered with Oonly to launch Hungary's first reusable PET bottle for mineral water - source: nextwavepartners.co.uk; cbinsights.com (2024/2025)

Customers

Beverage, food, and household-product brands globally, including mineral water and soft drink producers, breweries (via petainerKeg), and water-cooler operators (via petainerCooler) - source: petainer.com/about; nextwavepartners.co.uk

Partnerships

Oonly (Hungary reusable PET bottle launch, Jan 2024); named leader in the German refillable PET bottle market - source: cbinsights.com; nextwavepartners.co.uk

Circular Economy Activities

Reusable packaging (B2B, beverage kegs/bottles)
