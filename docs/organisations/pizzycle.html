Organisation Overview

Manufactures reusable, recyclable polypropylene pizza boxes (circular design, fits pizzas up to 13 inches) as a durable alternative to single-use cardboard pizza boxes for pizzerias and delivery chains.

Business Model

Reusable, stackable, dishwasher-safe pizza box made of 100% recyclable, PFAS-free material, reusable up to 500 times; the lid and bottom are identical pieces (halving manufacturing resource use), secured with a slide-lock and side ventilation to prevent sogginess. Operated via deposit-and-return systems: PIZZycle works both with existing reusable-packaging networks (e.g. Vytal, an app-based system showing participating pizzerias) and directly with individual restaurants running their own return system — source: interpack.com (2026); scottspizzatours.com (2022); pizzycle.com/sustainability

Customers

Pizzerias and pizza chains in Germany (and internationally, with reported customers/fans in Australia and Alaska), plus direct-to-consumer sales of PIZZycle bundles for home use — source: interpack.com (2026); pizzycle.com

Partnerships

Domino's Pizza Germany (piloting PIZZycle in select German locations as part of a broader reusable-packaging rollout plan); Vytal (integrated reusable-packaging network/app); DTU Copenhagen and Kleen hub (research partners for an independent Life Cycle Analysis of the box) — source: pizzycle.com/blog; interpack.com (2026); pizzycle.com/sustainability

Circular Economy Activities

Reusable packaging / Deposit-return

Measurable Impact

Pizza boxes reported to make up 22% of German takeaway waste and are typically non-recyclable due to grease contamination — the problem PIZZycle addresses; no independently audited company-specific waste-diversion figures were found in this pass, though a formal Life Cycle Analysis was conducted with DTU Copenhagen — source: pmq.com (2024); pizzycle.com/sustainability
