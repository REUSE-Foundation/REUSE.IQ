Organisation Overview

Organic grocery retailer in Al Manara, Dubai, stocking loose (unpackaged) organic produce to help shoppers avoid single-use plastic packaging.

Business Model

IMPORTANT CATEGORY CAVEAT: Ripe Organic is primarily an organic fresh-produce and grocery retailer/distributor (farm shops, weekly farmers markets, e-commerce, and last-mile delivery via InstaShop/Deliveroo/Careem) — it is not specifically a refill, reusable-packaging, deposit-return, or packaging-as-a-service operator in the sense the Foundation's criteria target. Its earlier candidate description ('plastic-free organic grocery') reflects a loose-produce retail model common to organic grocers generally, not a distinct reuse/refill system — source: ripeme.com; foodbusinessgulf.com (2026)

Customers

UAE consumers (retail and e-commerce) plus corporate/hospitality customers (Emirates Flight Catering, The Address Hotels, Jumeirah Bay Hotel, Common Grounds Cafe) and major retail partners (Spinneys, Carrefour, Union Coop, Abu Dhabi Coop, Al Ain Coop, SPAR, Lulu, Emirates Coop) — source: foodbusinessgulf.com (2026)

Partnerships

NRTC Group (acquirer, Dec 2025); Spinneys, Carrefour, Union Coop, Abu Dhabi Coop, Al Ain Coop, SPAR, Lulu, Emirates Coop (retail partners); Emirates Flight Catering, The Address Hotels, Jumeirah Bay Hotel, Common Grounds Cafe (hospitality/foodservice customers) — source: foodbusinessgulf.com (2026)
