Organisation Overview

Provides reusable food packaging plus industrial cleaning services for the food industry, including a rental service for reusable containers and a cleaning solution for food-service operators; serves restaurants, delivery platforms and caterers.

Business Model

Describes itself as 'the first returnable and reusable packaging service' in France: rents reusable containers to restaurants/caterers/delivery platforms and provides an industrial washing service. Uses a deposit model where end consumers pay a small amount when purchasing food in a returnable container, reimbursed when the container is returned to a delivery person or restaurant in the network, after which containers go through industrial washing for reuse — source: takagreen.com; cbinsights.com (citing a March 2021 'Discover 5 Top Circular Packaging Solutions' feature)
