Saruga Pack-Free Shopping Store

Package-free bulk shopping store noted as planning further Jakarta expansion; part of a small cluster of zero-waste bulk stores across Jakarta, Surabaya and Bali.

  • Est. company size: Small independent shop, founder-operated — source: waste4change.com

Organisation Overview

Package-free bulk shopping store noted as planning further Jakarta expansion; part of a small cluster of zero-waste bulk stores across Jakarta, Surabaya and Bali.

Business Model

Described as the first zero-waste store in South Jakarta: customers bring their own containers to buy 300+ items (staples, spices, cleaning products, personal-care items) by weight/gram; also sells lifestyle products like stainless-steel straws — source: nowjakarta.co.id (2022); flokq.com; goinggreeninjakarta.org

Customers

South Jakarta residents seeking package-free pantry staples, seasonings, personal care and home care products — source: nowjakarta.co.id (2022)
