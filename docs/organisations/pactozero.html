Organisation Overview

Platform providing returnable/reusable food packaging as a service to restaurants and food businesses: customers pay via a registered card or scannable code, have 15 days to return containers to a partner establishment or return point, and PactoZero (or a partner) handles cleaning and redistribution using durable polypropylene containers rated for 100+ reuses.

Business Model

Returnable/reusable packaging platform for takeaway food and grocery purchases, offering three distinct participation models: (1) registered users via the app with a refundable deposit-free system (only a sign-up bond, with a penalty if not returned within 15 days), (2) corporate/registered establishments as return points, and (3) anonymous/no-registration users who pay a per-container deposit at purchase and reclaim it via RFID-tracked automated return kiosks — source: pactozero.com/servicios; pactozero.com/como-funciona

Customers

Food-delivery and grocery/food-purchase establishments and their customers in Catalonia/Spain, described as the country's 'first reuse network' — source: pactozero.com/servicios

Partnerships

Rezero (Catalan NGO, partner in the 'Takeaway Retornable' / Reuse Vanguard Project initiative bringing PactoZero to Barcelona, 2025) — source: pactozero.com (Jun 2025)
