Organisation Overview

Health and wellness retailer on the Dolphin Coast/Durban offering plastic-free shopping for cosmetics, teas, face-care products and clothing, alongside natural cleaning products and biodegradable nappies.

Business Model

Originally opened as a natural and organic emporium; over time expanded its plastic-free product range as the founders became more aware of waste-reduction needs — not an exclusively zero-waste shop, but a growing plastic-free offering within a broader health/wellness retail model — source: dailymaverick.co.za (2019)

Customers

Ballito/Durban-area residents seeking natural, organic and increasingly plastic-free products — source: dailymaverick.co.za (2019)
