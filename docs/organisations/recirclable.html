Organisation Overview

App-based reusable-container network for takeout: customers download the app, pre-authorise payment (only charged if containers aren't returned), then order via 'Recirclable' at participating restaurants or scan a QR code at checkout to borrow reusable containers.

Business Model

App-based reusable-container borrowing system for restaurant takeout: customers download the free app (payment details collected but only charged for unreturned containers), scan a QR code at a participating restaurant to borrow containers (durable Dutch-made 'Cirqula' containers by Mepal, BPA-free polypropylene, rated for ~1,000 uses), and return them to any participating restaurant within 14 days to avoid fees; restaurants clean/sanitize containers in commercial dishwashers for reuse. Partners with nonprofit programmes like LexZeroWaste (Lexington) and Rethink Disposable (Northampton) to reach new restaurants at no upfront cost to the business for the first year — source: recirclable.com; recirclable.com/faq; lexobserver.org (2025); westernmassnews.com (2025)

Customers

Restaurants and cafes across Massachusetts (Arlington, Boston, Lexington, Northampton) and their takeout customers — source: greennewton.org; westernmassnews.com (Dec 2025); lexobserver.org (May 2025)

Partnerships

LexZeroWaste (nonprofit partner in Lexington, connecting Recirclable with local businesses including Royal India Bistro and Revival Cafe); Rethink Disposable (partner programme in Northampton) — source: lexobserver.org (2025); westernmassnews.com (2025)

Measurable Impact

One partner restaurant example (per a 2022 profile): 300 single-use bowls saved from the trash by investing in just 30 reusable bowls — a 10x reuse ratio illustrating the model's efficiency; no broader, network-wide audited figures were found in this pass — source: greennewton.org (2022)
