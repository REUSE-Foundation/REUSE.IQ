Organisation Overview

Sri Lankan technology company that partnered with Unilever in 2019 to install high-tech refill machines dispensing Dove, Sunsilk and Sunlight products in modern-trade supermarket outlets, targeting reduction of flexible plastic sachets.

Business Model

IMPORTANT CONTEXT: Vega Innovations' primary business is manufacturing electric vehicles (it built South Asia's first electric supercar, the Vega EVX) and EV components (inverters, drivetrains, battery packs) — the refill/reuse business is a subsidiary product line called 'UFill', Sri Lanka's first locally-manufactured liquid vending/dispensing machine. UFill machines let consumers bring their own bottle to refill household/personal-care liquids (shampoo, soap, detergent) in modern-trade retail settings, reducing single-use sachet/bottle consumption — source: vega.lk; ufill.net; srilankabusiness.com

Customers

Modern-trade supermarkets and shopping centres in Sri Lanka and Bangladesh, dispensing refills of Unilever brands (Dove, Lifebuoy, Sunsilk, Sunlight, Vim) — source: ft.lk (Oct 2024); sundaytimes.lk

Partnerships

Unilever Sri Lanka (long-running partner since a 2019 pilot, formalised via an October 2024 MoU with USAID's Ocean Plastics Reduction Activity to scale up refill stations for Dove, Lifebuoy, Sunsilk, Sunlight, Vim); Unimart (Bangladesh shopping centre deployment) — source: ft.lk (Oct 2024); unilever.com (Feb 2025); sundaytimes.lk

Circular Economy Activities

Refill (dispenser/hardware technology provider)

Measurable Impact

The UFill/Unilever/USAID scale-up initiative is projected to prevent over 128 metric tons of plastic waste from entering Sri Lankan landfills by 2027, with consumers benefiting from 20-30% lower costs versus buying new packaging — these are forward-looking targets from an October 2024 announcement, not yet-achieved results — source: ft.lk (Oct 2024)
