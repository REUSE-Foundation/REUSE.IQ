Organisation Overview

Nonprofit/operator running a reusable cup and container network for coffee shops and foodservice, enabling customers to borrow and return standardized reusable packaging at partner locations.

Business Model

Free, tech-enabled reusable stainless-steel cup-share service ('Freeturnable™') for cafes: customers sign up (with a credit card charged only if a cup isn't returned), check out a cup via app/QR code, and return it to any participating cafe or return kiosk within the network — source: vesselworks.org/faq; dailycal.org

Customers

Cafes and coffee shops in Boulder, Colorado (8 food vendors + University of Colorado campus) and Berkeley, California (11 food/beverage vendors, won municipal RFP) — source: greenbrownblue.com; dailycal.org (2019)

Partnerships

Boxcar Coffee Roasters (named Boulder pilot cafe); University of Colorado (CU) campus; City of Berkeley (won municipal RFP, 2019) — source: fastcompany.com; greenbrownblue.com

Circular Economy Activities

Deposit-return / Reusable packaging

Measurable Impact

Boulder, first 6 months of operation (~2018-2019): 4,500+ Vessel cups checked out, 1,260 users signed up, 85% repeat-use rate — source: greenbrownblue.com (2021 profile, republished 2024 context)
