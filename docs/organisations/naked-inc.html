Organisation Overview

Zero-waste grocery store at COMO Park, Kemang, South Jakarta, selling eco-friendly goods and locally-sourced groceries (some organic/handmade) in bulk dispensers for container-free shopping.

Business Model

Described as 'the first #ZeroWaste health store in Jakarta' (per its own Instagram bio); customers bring their own jars, mesh bags, bottles, or containers to buy food ingredients (pasta, rice, seeds) and personal-care products in bulk; open daily 8am-8pm — source: instagram.com/nakedincjkt; thejakartapost.com (2020)
