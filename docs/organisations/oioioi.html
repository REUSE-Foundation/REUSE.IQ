Organisation Overview

Rental service for organic baby clothing, allowing parents to size up as children grow rather than buying and discarding garments.

Business Model

Subscription-based rental of organic baby clothing bundles (MINI: 5 pieces, MIDI: 10, MAXI: 15, covering ~25-75% of wardrobe needs) sourced from ~10 small, GOTS-certified sustainable/organic brands; customers swap for the next size whenever their baby outgrows the current set (average cycle ~52 days/7 weeks in a baby's first year), with free delivery and returns; customers may also purchase a piece they wish to keep, with the rental fee deducted — source: oioioibaby.ch/babykleidermieten; wemakeit.com

Customers

Parents of babies aged 0-12 months (sizes 44-92) in Switzerland, expanding to the rest of Europe — source: tracxn.com; linkedin.com/company/oioioirent

Partnerships

Jelmoli (Zurich department store, hosted an OiOiOi sustainability pop-up as part of its 'Green Act' strategy); Belgian brand hvid (co-branded newborn giftbox); ~10 unnamed sustainable clothing-brand suppliers — source: igds.org (2022); oioioibaby.ch
