Organisation Overview

Digital directory/platform for Berlin's repair economy: helps residents find certified repair workshops (with transparent pricing and pickup/delivery), book device rentals, offload large appliances for reuse, and locate local Repair Café events.

Business Model

Online directory/booking platform connecting Berliners with certified local electronics repair shops (comparing cost, guarantee, turnaround time), complementing (not replacing) Berlin's volunteer-run Repair Café network; also runs a Repair Café Finder covering 26+ Berlin locations, and a separate B2B IT Asset Disposition (ITAD) service adding repair/refurbishment (rather than only resale/recycling) for business device fleets, including on-site pickup and data-erasure/impact reporting — source: repaircircle.eu (multiple pages, 2026); trustpilot.com

Customers

Berlin residents seeking electronics repair (laptops, smartphones, tablets); Berlin businesses for IT Asset Disposition (ITAD)-style repair/refurbishment of fleets, from single co-working tenants to full corporate fleets — source: the-berliner.com (2024); repaircircle.eu/business

Partnerships

Berlin Senate Department for Mobility, Transport, Climate Protection and the Environment (via the 'RepairBONUS' subsidy programme for household electronics repair); referenced alongside Berlin's independent Repair Café network and 'teilbar' (Berlin's peer-to-peer tool-sharing platform) as complementary parts of the city's repair ecosystem — source: trustpilot.com; repaircircle.eu/en/news/berlin-repair-revolution (2026)
