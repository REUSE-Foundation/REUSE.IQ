Organisation Overview

Regional reusable cup network ('Colorado Cup'): customers borrow a cup for a small deposit from any participating café and can return it to any café in the network for a discount on their next order — no app or bank-card link required.

Business Model

'Borrow and reuse' deposit system: customers pay a $2.50 deposit for a durable, dishwasher-safe polypropylene cup (rated for 1,000+ uses, available in 12oz/16oz hot and cold formats), and receive a $2.50 discount (not cash) when they return it to any participating cafe in the network, regardless of where it was borrowed — source: retrocycle.org/faq; summitdaily.com (2026)

Customers

Cafes and coffee shops in Colorado mountain towns (Breckenridge, expanding to Frisco) and their customers/tourists — source: summitdaily.com (2026); townofbreckenridge.com

Partnerships

Named Breckenridge cafes: The Crown, Breckenridge Coffee House, LoLo Juice, Cool River Coffee House, Mountain View Coffee Bar, Ullr Cafe (Grand Colorado on Peak 8), Semplice Café, Amazing Grace; Frisco: Rocky Mountain Coffee Roasters, Mountain Dweller Coffee Roasters; cup co-designed with local artist Nikki LaRochelle; partnership with Town of Frisco's sustainability team — source: townofbreckenridge.com; summitdaily.com

Circular Economy Activities

Deposit-return

Measurable Impact

Prompted by a 2023 Breckenridge waste audit finding coffee cups made up nearly one-third of all downtown trash by weight; survey found ~90% of visitors expressed interest in a reusable-cup option. No post-launch waste-diversion figures yet available, as the programme only launched in May 2026 — source: summitdaily.com (May 2026)
