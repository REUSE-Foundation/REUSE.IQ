Ecandi (Eco Bulk Bar Refill Station)

Operates Kenya's first bulk refill station, in Nairobi's Rosslyn area, where customers bring their own containers to refill ethically sourced household and personal-care products from partner producers.

Organisation Overview

Operates Kenya's first bulk refill station, in Nairobi's Rosslyn area, where customers bring their own containers to refill ethically sourced household and personal-care products from partner producers.

Business Model

Described as Kenya's first bulk refill station ('Eco Bulk Bar'), located within a broader zero-waste eco-shop; customers bring their own clean, empty containers (or purchase a recycled container on-site at minimal cost) to refill handpicked cleaning, personal-care, and laundry products sourced from partner producers — source: ecandi.co.ke/refill

Customers

Nairobi residents seeking zero-waste/sustainable household, personal-care, cleaning and laundry products — source: ecandi.co.ke/refill

Partnerships

Nairobi Circular Economy ClimAccelerator (selected for its 2025 Circular Business Cohort) — source: circularacceleration.com
