Business Model

Based solely on the company's own website tagline, Battmen appears to offer a battery-swap model: users exchange depleted battery packs for charged ones at docking/swap stations, avoiding the need to own a dedicated charger or wait for a battery to charge — a model comparable to e-bike or small-device battery-swap services seen elsewhere. However, no further detail on target devices (e.g., e-bikes, power tools, consumer electronics), pricing, subscription structure, station locations, or count of stations was found in verifiable public sources.
