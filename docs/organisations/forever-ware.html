Organisation Overview

Returnable takeout container service working with roughly 20 independent restaurant and coffee-shop partners across multiple US markets (starting in Minneapolis, expanding to New Hampshire), replacing single-use takeout packaging with a reusable, returnable system.

Business Model

Reusable takeout container service: restaurants/coffee shops pay $25-200/month to license Forever Ware's tracking software plus 5 cents per checkout; customers check out and return stainless-steel containers and mugs (with ID tags) via a self-serve 'smart return station' modeled on library self-checkout kiosks — source: barandrestaurant.com (2022); cbinsights.com

Customers

Restaurants and coffee shops in the Twin Cities (Minneapolis-St. Paul) area, e.g. named customer Namaste Cafe — source: cbinsights.com (citing Star Tribune, Nov 2022)

Partnerships

Namaste Cafe (named 17-year-old restaurant customer); Minnesota DEED (grant funder) — source: cbinsights.com (2022)
