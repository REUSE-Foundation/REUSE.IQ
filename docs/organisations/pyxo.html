Organisation Overview

B2B marketplace and reverse-logistics platform for reusable food containers: fast-food chains and restaurants buy batches of QR-code or NFC-tagged reusable containers, and Pyxo coordinates outsourced cleaning/collection partners who scan and redistribute clean containers across the network.

Business Model

B2B marketplace and reverse-logistics platform for reusable food containers: restaurants/chains buy batches of containers tagged with QR codes or NFC chips; customers can return containers to any network collection point (not necessarily the original restaurant) via an app; Pyxo positioned itself as a neutral 'network operator' analogous to a utility grid — source: techcrunch.com (2021)

Customers

Fast-food chains, restaurants, catering services (initially Sodexo), meal-delivery/foodtech companies (Foodles, Popchef named), and event caterers in France — source: techcrunch.com (2021); cbinsights.com

Partnerships

Sodexo (early corporate catering partner); Foodles and Popchef (connected-fridge foodtech partners); an unnamed French fast-food chain (per TechCrunch, 2021); investors Eurazeo, Five Seasons Ventures, Alliance for Impact, and Ubi Solutions — source: techcrunch.com; cbinsights.com

Measurable Impact
