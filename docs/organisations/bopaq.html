Organisation Overview

Reusable food and beverage packaging system (cups and food boxes rated for 1,000+ washes) for restaurants, caterers, institutional cafeterias, grocery stores, and events, with an optional app-based reward system to incentivize returns. Founded by Mishel Wong, a former supplier of dish-washing equipment to the foodservice industry.

Business Model

Reusable food and beverage packaging service (cups and food boxes rated for 1,000+ washes) for restaurants, caterers, institutional cafeterias, grocery stores and events; app-based system scans container and personal code to track borrowing, free to use if returned within 14 days; co-founded Canada's 'National Wash Network' (with ShareWares, Earthware and Muuse) providing shared commercial washing/logistics infrastructure nationwide — source: plasticstoday.com; globalnews.ca; linkedin.com/company/bo-reusable-takeout-packaging-service

Customers

Restaurants, caterers, institutional cafeterias, event organisers, grocery stores, film-industry catering, and retirement homes in the Montreal/Quebec region — source: plasticstoday.com (2025); globalnews.ca (2023)

Partnerships

Tricentris Coop (acquirer, 2026); National Wash Network co-founders ShareWares, Earthware, Muuse; Lufa Farms (power-wash service client); Vegâteau (named restaurant customer) — source: betakit.com; globalnews.ca; linkedin.com

Circular Economy Activities

Reusable packaging / Packaging-as-a-Service

Measurable Impact

Over 2.4 million reusable containers processed since 2021 launch (as of March 2025); in its first year, containers borrowed 3,000+ times across 30 participating restaurants in Montreal — source: liveyourpotential.ca (2025); globalnews.ca (2023)
